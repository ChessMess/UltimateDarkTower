Adds the tower's sound library: `@udtc/assets/audio` (113 official samples, filename → URL) and
`@udtc/assets/audio-effects` (the two hand-maintained clips, `drumCalibration` and `drumRotation`).
With these, the package now owns every art and audio byte in the monorepo.

Unlike `/tokens`, these use per-file `new URL(literal, import.meta.url)` rather than
`import.meta.glob` — deliberately, and the two are **not** interchangeable. Verified directly:
under Vite's library mode a glob resolves to a static, base-less string
(`/Adversary_Ashstrider_01.ogg`), while the `new URL` form survives into the emitted bundle and
stays relative to the chunk at runtime. `packages/display` is a library, so only the latter lets
each consuming app's bundler re-detect and re-emit the files.

`src/audio/index.ts` is generated by `packages/display/scripts/extract-audio.mjs`; hand-maintained
sounds go in `src/audio/effects.ts`, which the generator never touches.
